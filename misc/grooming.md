# Morning

1. Floss, brush, and use tongue scraper. 
2. Rinse face thoroughly with lukewarm water.[^water]
3. Apply Hada Labo Gokujyun Premium Lotion to damp skin.[^humectant]
4. Apply CeraVe Ultra-Light Moisturizing Lotion SPF 50 evenly over the face and neck.[^cerave_am]

[^water]: Avoiding alkaline cleansers in the morning prevents the unnecessary stripping of the skin's natural lipid barrier.

[^humectant]: Dispense a coin-sized amount and pat into the face. Hyaluronic acid acts as a humectant, meaning it binds to water. Applying to a dry face will just draw moisture out from the deeper layers of the skin, exacerbating dryness.

[^cerave_am]: This functions as both the occlusive seal for the humectant and daytime UV protection. Layering an additional lotion underneath is redundant and may cause product pilling.

# Daytime

**1. Hands**

Apply CoenRich Q10 Deep Moisture Cream after every hand wash.[^niacinamide]

[^niacinamide]: Consistent application of Niacinamide inhibits melanin transfer and softens texture. Frequent application is required to see physical changes to the skin over time.

**2. Odor**

Apply Deonatulle Soft Stone W to clean, dry underarms.[^alum]

[^alum]: Alum is an astringent that neutralizes odor-causing bacteria. It must be applied to completely dry skin to be effective.

# Night

**1. Deep Cleansing Shower**

Wash body and shampoo hair using the ETVOS Relaxing Massage Brush.[^etvos]

[^etvos]: Lather shampoo and press the silicone brush against the scalp in small scrubbing motions. Mechanical exfoliation removes buildup more thoroughly than fingers.

**2. Nighttime Recovery**

On the face:

1. Wash with SENKA Perfect Whip.[^foam]
2. Apply Hada Labo Lotion to damp skin.
3. Apply Lubriderm to seal in hydration.
4. Wait 2-3 minutes for the lotion to completely set.
5. Apply 1 pump of CeraVe Resurfacing Retinol Serum.[^retinol]

Finally, apply a heavy layer of CoenRich Q10 to the hands.

[^foam]: Squeeze 2cm into palms and lather with water. Foaming the cleanser prevents excessive physical rubbing, protecting the skin barrier.

[^retinol]: Applying retinol over a dried layer of moisturizer (buffering) slows its absorption rate. This bypasses severe irritation and barrier damage while allowing the encapsulated active ingredient to accelerate cell turnover.
