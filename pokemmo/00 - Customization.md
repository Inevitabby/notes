# Bindings

## Hotbar

**Overworld**:
- `<Spacebar>`: Bike
- `s`: Sweet Scent
- `m`: Map

**Battle**:
- `1`: Pokeball

## Movement

Binding movement to `hjkl`:
1. Results in easier resting position (especially on a tiny keyboard), and
2. Makes spacebar a natural leader key (and reduces travel distance to other keys)
