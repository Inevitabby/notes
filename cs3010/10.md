# Euler's Method

$$
\boxed{
	y(x_{i+1}) = y(x_i) + h f(x,y)
} \\
\small\textit{remember, $x_{i+1} = x_{i+h}$}
$$

> **Note** — Euler's only works on first-order linear differential equations. In other words, it [must]{.underline} be of the form:
> 
> $$
> y'(x) = f(x,y)
> $$

> **Note** — This algorithm is really sensitive to $h$ (you want to use a small $h$) because we're truncating the Taylor series.
