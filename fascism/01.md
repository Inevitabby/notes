# What is Fascism?

Fascism is a revolutionary[^1] nationalist[^5], anti-liberal, sexist[^2], and violent[^3] mass political movement on the right-wing.
- Requires a large, organized group of people.
- Interested in preserving power of people they think should be in charge (doesn't care about liberating the oppressed).
- Believes other people don't belong in their communities and are less-worthy of rights and privileges (or that they aren't people at all and should be exterminated).
- Doesn't play by the rules.[^4]
- Power base is overwhelmingly young men who belong to whatever ethnic, racial, or religious group is the majority in their country.
- Tends to be authoritarian.

[^1]: As in aiming to remake the world and believing that the current system of government and how society works in general must be completely changed. (e.g., rewriting laws and transforming government from the ground up (one of the few traits that fascists share with the radical left)).

[^2]: Such as believing that there are natural environments for men and not for women (e.g., relegation of women to the world of family and childcare) and that women shouldn't have or want social power.

[^3]: As in viewing violence as good (rather than just useful). (e.g., violence makes men into better men and violent men produce better futures). While other political groups have used violence as a means to an end, violence is the goal for fascists and a major part of their revolutionary ideology.

[^4]: As in being willing to do whatever they believe is necessary to maintain power. This means a combination of normal electoral politics and organizing, *and* political violence. 

[^5]: As in, they think about politics in terms of nations (defined by religion, ethnicity, or race) rather than classes or interest groups. They believe that their nation is the best in the world.

# Fascism & The Right-Wing

**Conservatives use fascists as a hit squad to do the dirty work**[^DIRTY] their supporters won't or don't want to be connected to.[^CRISIS]

[^CRISIS]: In times of crisis, the mainstream right-wing looks to fascists as a way to keep the left and center in line. Other times, conservatives discard the fascists once they've served their purpose.

> **Example**: Historical Examples
> 
> - Nazi paramilitaries helped German conservatives stop union and Communist organizing.
> - The KKK was a tool of the Southern white establishment.

**Fascists use conservatives to gain momentum and [legitimacy]{.underline}**. They intentionally blur the differences between their movement and mainstream conservatives.[^FACTIONS]

[^DIRTY]: Street fights, assassinations, and intimidation.


[^FACTIONS]: Since the 1940s, fascists have tended to stay smaller and form factions inside of bigger right-wing parties.

# Why Become a Fascist?

For fascists, fascism is a community and a force for good[^FORGOOD]. Fascism is on the rise throughout the world and it appeals to young men because it claims to be able to improve their lives[^ANDITCAN]. Being an early party member in Germany and Italy meant job opportunities, government contracts, prestige, and priviledge. Many people wanted to be fascists, fascism was (and is) popular.

[^FORGOOD]: Hitler didn't win power advocating for mass murder, he won power by blaming Jewish people and Communists for Germany's problems and proposed a new national unity based on white, Aryan identity.

[^ANDITCAN]: And it can! Fascist parties in the 1920—1940s materially improved the lives of their followers by reorganizing economies, capitalizing on the improving world economy, and stealing from the people they looked down on.
